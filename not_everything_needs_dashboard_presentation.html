The so what rule

Keep asking "so what?" until something can be done.

DataRevenue decreased 8%.
So what?Mainly a 12% drop in corporate trips.
So what?Three corporate accounts reduced usage.
So what?Their fulfilment got worse.
So what?Driver availability was low in their peak booking windows.
ActionFix driver supply around those accounts.
